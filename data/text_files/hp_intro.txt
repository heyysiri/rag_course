Harry Potter is a wizard who discovers at the age of eleven that he has magical abilities and has been accepted into Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. He is the son of James and Lily Potter and is raised by his aunt Petunia Dursley and her family before learning about the wizarding world. Harry is famous for surviving an attack from Lord Voldemort as a baby, although the event leaves him with a lightning-shaped scar on his forehead.

At Hogwarts, Harry becomes close friends with Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger. Ron comes from a large wizarding family and is known for his loyalty, humor, and love of chess. Hermione is exceptionally studious and often solves problems by researching spells, magical creatures, and historical information. The three friends frequently work together when they encounter mysterious events at Hogwarts.

Harry is sorted into Gryffindor House, along with Ron and Hermione. Other notable Gryffindor students include Neville Longbottom and Ginny Weasley. Draco Malfoy, one of Harry's rivals at school, belongs to Slytherin. Draco often competes with Harry and has a complicated relationship with his family, particularly his father Lucius Malfoy.

Hogwarts itself contains many memorable locations. The Great Hall is where students eat meals and attend important ceremonies. The library contains a huge collection of magical books, while the Forbidden Forest is home to creatures such as centaurs, unicorns, and Acromantulas. The Room of Requirement is a mysterious room that appears when someone has a genuine need for it.

Harry plays Quidditch, a popular wizarding sport played on flying broomsticks. He becomes a Seeker for the Gryffindor team. The objective of the Seeker is to catch the Golden Snitch, a small flying golden ball. Other Quidditch positions include Chasers, Beaters, and the Keeper.

Several magical objects are important to Harry and his friends. Harry uses the Marauder's Map to explore Hogwarts secretly. Hermione uses a Time-Turner during her third year to attend multiple classes. Harry also receives an Invisibility Cloak that originally belonged to his father. Ron's most memorable magical object may be his broken-down wand, which causes problems during his second year.

The trio's adventures involve many other characters, including Hagrid, the gamekeeper of Hogwarts; Professor McGonagall, the Transfiguration professor; Albus Dumbledore, the headmaster; and Sirius Black, Harry's godfather. Hagrid is particularly fond of magical creatures and often introduces Harry, Ron, and Hermione to unusual animals.

Harry's story ultimately revolves around his conflict with Voldemort, but friendship is one of its strongest recurring themes. Harry, Ron, and Hermione repeatedly rely on each other's different strengths. Ron's bravery and loyalty, Hermione's knowledge and preparation, and Harry's determination often complement one another when they face dangerous situations.
