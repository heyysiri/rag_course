Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry is a magical boarding school located somewhere in the Scottish Highlands. It is one of the most famous wizarding schools in the world and is where young witches and wizards learn about magic, spells, potions, magical creatures, and wizarding history.

Students typically begin attending Hogwarts at the age of eleven and spend seven years studying there. When new students arrive, they are sorted into one of four houses: Gryffindor, Hufflepuff, Ravenclaw, or Slytherin. Each house has its own traditions, common room, and values, and students earn or lose points for their behavior and achievements throughout the school year.

The castle itself is enormous and filled with enchanted rooms, moving staircases, secret passages, magical paintings, and mysterious corridors. The Great Hall is the center of school life, where students eat meals beneath an enchanted ceiling that reflects the sky outside. Hogwarts also has classrooms for subjects such as Transfiguration, Charms, Potions, Defense Against the Dark Arts, Herbology, Astronomy, and History of Magic.

Outside the castle are the Hogwarts grounds, including the Quidditch pitch, the greenhouses, the Black Lake, and the Forbidden Forest. The forest is officially off-limits to students, although Harry Potter and his friends have a habit of finding reasons to enter it.

Hogwarts is more than just a school. For Harry Potter, Ron Weasley, Hermione Granger, and many other students, it becomes a place where they form lifelong friendships, discover their abilities, encounter strange mysteries, and experience some of the most important events of their lives.
